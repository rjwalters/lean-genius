Cross-section observations that no single line comment owns.

## The failure mode this fixture exists to demonstrate

All twenty-two points carried by rigor, evidence sufficiency,
reproducibility, and citation hygiene are awarded in full. The paper is
correct. It is also, on the evidence of its own abstract, about nothing in
particular: it reports that a distribution is skewed and that a prototype
built on that skew helped, and it declines to say what either fact means.

Before the dim 3 / dim 9 symmetry shipped (issue #1046/#1047), this paper
scored like a solid submission — the rubric asked whether the contribution was
*unambiguous*, and a narrow contribution stated clearly passes that test. What
it did not ask was whether the stated contribution was the strongest honest
one the work supports. The thread brief answers that question directly, and
the paper does not match its own brief.

## Symmetry check (the other direction)

No overclaiming was found, and the reviewer explicitly did not reward its
absence. Caution is not a credit here: the rubric treats the two failure modes
as symmetric, so a paper that is safe from overclaiming by refusing to claim
anything gets no points for the refusal.
