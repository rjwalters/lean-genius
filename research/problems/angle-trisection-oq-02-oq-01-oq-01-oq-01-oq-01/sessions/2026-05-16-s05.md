## §3. Mathematical content of Step 1a

**Statement** (paste-ready, target placement is just after the `def aGen` block in the existing Lean file, around line 80):

```
/-- `aGen` is not a square in `base = FractionRing (Polynomial (ZMod 2))`.
    Proof: a square `(p/q)² = aGen` would force `p² = X · q²` in
    `Polynomial (ZMod 2)`. Taking natDegree on both sides gives an even
    number = odd number, contradiction. -/
lemma aGen_not_isSquare : ¬ IsSquare aGen := …
```

**Mathematical proof** (target Lean realization is in §4):

Let `R := Polynomial (ZMod 2)` and `K := FractionRing R = base`. Both `R` and `K` carry the standard `IsFractionRing R K` instance. `R` is a UFD (polynomial ring over a field), `X = Polynomial.X` is an irreducible element of `R` (in fact the unique monic irreducible of degree 1 with constant term 0).

Suppose `y : K` with `y * y = aGen = algebraMap R K X`. By `IsFractionRing.div_surjective` (Mathlib v4.26.0 in `Mathlib.RingTheory.Localization.FractionRing`) there exist `p : R` and `q : R⁰` (the non-zero-divisors submonoid of `R`) such that `y = IsLocalization.mk' K p q`. Then `IsLocalization.mk' K p q * IsLocalization.mk' K p q = algebraMap R K X` rewrites via `IsLocalization.mk'_mul`, `IsLocalization.mk'_eq_iff_eq_mul`, and field-of-fractions injectivity (or, equivalently, multiplication by `q²` and `IsFractionRing.injective`) to

```
p * p = X * (q.val * q.val)  in  Polynomial (ZMod 2).
```

This is an equation in the UFD `R`. Taking `Polynomial.natDegree` of both sides — both sides nonzero (`q ≠ 0` since `q ∈ R⁰`; LHS would force `p = 0`, hence `y = 0`, but `aGen ≠ 0` since `algebraMap` is injective on a domain and `X ≠ 0`) — gives, via `Polynomial.natDegree_mul`:

```
2 * p.natDegree  =  1 + 2 * q.val.natDegree.
```

LHS is even; RHS is `1 + (even)` = odd. No natural number is both even and odd, contradiction (`Nat.even_iff_not_odd` + `Nat.even_two_mul` + `Nat.odd_iff_not_even`).

**Remarks**:

- The argument uses only that `Polynomial (ZMod 2)` is a domain with `natDegree X = 1` (any field would do). The char-2 hypothesis is irrelevant for Step 1a — it only enters in Step 1b (Artin-Schreier irreducibility of `g_factor`).
- A more abstract version of this lemma exists for any DVR-style setup: if `π` is an irreducible element of a UFD `R` with `IsFractionRing R K`, then `π` is not a square in `K`. We do *not* attempt to generalize in this PREP — the slug-specific version is shorter and avoids a Mathlib-search detour.

/-- `aGen ≠ 0` in `base`: the algebraMap from a domain to its FractionRing is
    injective, and `Polynomial.X ≠ 0`. -/
lemma aGen_ne_zero : aGen ≠ 0 := by
  unfold aGen
  -- `algebraMap R (FractionRing R)` is injective on the domain `Polynomial (ZMod 2)`.
  -- `Polynomial.X ≠ 0` because its natDegree is 1.
  rw [Ne, ← (algebraMap (Polynomial (ZMod 2)) base).map_zero,
      Function.Injective.eq_iff (IsFractionRing.injective (Polynomial (ZMod 2)) base)]
  exact Polynomial.X_ne_zero

/-- Internal helper: in a UFD with `IsFractionRing R K`, if `p² = X · q²` in `R`
    with `q ≠ 0` (and hence `p ≠ 0`), then `natDegree`-parity gives a contradiction.
    Specialized to `R = Polynomial (ZMod 2)`. -/
private lemma R_sq_eq_X_mul_sq_imp_false
    {p q : Polynomial (ZMod 2)} (hq : q ≠ 0) (hpq : p * p = Polynomial.X * (q * q)) :
    False := by
  -- Both sides are nonzero.
  have hX : (Polynomial.X : Polynomial (ZMod 2)) ≠ 0 := Polynomial.X_ne_zero
  have hpp_ne : p * p ≠ 0 := by
    rw [hpq]; exact mul_ne_zero hX (mul_ne_zero hq hq)
  have hp : p ≠ 0 := fun h => hpp_ne (by rw [h, zero_mul])
  -- natDegree on both sides.
  have hLHS : (p * p).natDegree = 2 * p.natDegree := by
    rw [Polynomial.natDegree_mul hp hp]; ring
  have hRHS : (Polynomial.X * (q * q)).natDegree = 1 + 2 * q.natDegree := by
    rw [Polynomial.natDegree_mul hX (mul_ne_zero hq hq),
        Polynomial.natDegree_X, Polynomial.natDegree_mul hq hq]
    ring
  have hEq : 2 * p.natDegree = 1 + 2 * q.natDegree := by
    have := congrArg Polynomial.natDegree hpq
    rw [hLHS, hRHS] at this; exact this
  -- LHS even, RHS odd → contradiction.
  have hL_even : Even (2 * p.natDegree) := ⟨p.natDegree, by ring⟩
  have hR_odd  : Odd  (1 + 2 * q.natDegree) := ⟨q.natDegree, by ring⟩
  rw [hEq] at hL_even
  exact (Nat.not_odd_iff_even.mpr hL_even) hR_odd

/-- **Step 1a**: `aGen` is not a square in `base = FractionRing (Polynomial (ZMod 2))`.

    Proof: a putative square `y² = aGen` rewrites — via the standard `IsFractionRing`
    surjectivity onto `mk' K p q` and clearing denominators — to `p² = X · q²` in
    `Polynomial (ZMod 2)`. Taking `natDegree` gives `2 · natDegree p = 1 + 2 · natDegree q`,
    forcing an even number = odd number, contradiction. -/
lemma aGen_not_isSquare : ¬ IsSquare aGen := by
  rintro ⟨y, hy⟩
  -- Get numerator/denominator of y in Polynomial (ZMod 2).
  obtain ⟨⟨p, q⟩, hyq⟩ := IsLocalization.surj (M := (Polynomial (ZMod 2))⁰) (S := base) y
  -- hyq : y * (algebraMap _ _ q.val) = algebraMap _ _ p
  have hqv_ne : q.val ≠ 0 := nonZeroDivisors.coe_ne_zero q
  -- Multiply hy by (q.val)² to clear denominators:
  -- y² · q² = aGen · q²  ⟹  (y · q)² = aGen · q²  ⟹  p² = X · q²
  -- We use that hyq lets us substitute y · q with p (via algebraMap).
  -- TODO(S6 ACT, SORRY-1): bridge from `y * y = aGen` + `y · algebraMap _ _ q = algebraMap _ _ p`
  -- to `p * p = X * (q.val * q.val)` in `Polynomial (ZMod 2)`. The bridge is
  -- ≈ 6-12 LOC of `IsFractionRing.injective` + `algebraMap` ring-hom rewrites:
  --   1. From `hy : y * y = aGen`, derive `(y * y) * (algebraMap _ _ q.val * algebraMap _ _ q.val) = aGen * (algebraMap _ _ q.val * algebraMap _ _ q.val)`.
  --   2. Regroup LHS as `(y * algebraMap _ _ q.val) * (y * algebraMap _ _ q.val)` and apply hyq twice.
  --   3. Result is `algebraMap _ _ (p * p) = algebraMap _ _ (X * (q.val * q.val))` (using `aGen = algebraMap _ _ X` by def).
  --   4. Apply `IsFractionRing.injective`.
  -- This is mechanical; ACT in S6 will close it.
  have hpq : p * p = Polynomial.X * (q.val * q.val) := by
    sorry  -- SORRY-1 (S6 ACT target, R3 mechanical bridge ~6-12 LOC)
  exact R_sq_eq_X_mul_sq_imp_false hqv_ne hpq
