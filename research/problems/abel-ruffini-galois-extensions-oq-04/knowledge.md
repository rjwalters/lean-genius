## Problem Understanding

The Jordan-Hölder theorem states that composition series of a finite group are unique
up to permutation and isomorphism of factors. Mathlib has `CompositionSeries` and
`JordanHolderModule` infrastructure. The goal is to expose this as an explicit named
theorem and instantiate it for the S₄ composition chain used in the Abel-Ruffini proof.

Key Mathlib modules: `Mathlib.GroupTheory.CompositionSeries`, `Mathlib.GroupTheory.JordanHolder`.
