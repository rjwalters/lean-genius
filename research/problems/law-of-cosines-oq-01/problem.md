# Spherical Law of Cosines

## Source
- **Proof**: law-of-cosines (`The spherical Law of Cosines cos c = cos a cos b + sin a sin b cos C`)
- **Category**: extension/generalization
- **Tractability**: tractable

## Problem Description

Formalize the spherical law of cosines in Lean 4:
  cos(c) = cos(a)·cos(b) + sin(a)·sin(b)·cos(C)

for a spherical triangle on the unit sphere S² with arc-length sides a, b, c
and dihedral angle C opposite side c.
