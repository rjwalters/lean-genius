### Formal Statement
$$
For lattice triangles $T_1, T_2$ sharing an edge $e$ with no interior lattice points, the area functional is additive: $\mathrm{Area}(T_1 \cup T_2) = \mathrm{Area}(T_1) + \mathrm{Area}(T_2)$, and consequently $I(T_1 \cup T_2) + \tfrac{1}{2}B(T_1 \cup T_2) - 1 = \big(I(T_1)+\tfrac12 B(T_1)-1\big) + \big(I(T_2)+\tfrac12 B(T_2)-1\big)$, the additivity step in the triangulation proof of Pick's theorem.
$$
