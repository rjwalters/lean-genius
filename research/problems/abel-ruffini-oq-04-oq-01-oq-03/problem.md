# Problem: Generic Sylow Lemma for Galois Group Elimination

**Status**: Active

## Problem Statement

### Formal Statement

$$
\text{Generic Sylow elimination: if } G \leq S_5 \text{ is transitive, } |G| = k \in \{10, 20, 40\},
\text{ and } G \text{ has a unique Sylow 5-subgroup, then } \sigma \in G \text{ a transposition implies contradiction.}
$$

Abstractly: formalize a lemma of the form

$$
\texttt{sylow\_elim\_card} : \forall G : \text{Subgroup}(S_5),\ G.\text{isTransitive} \to \sigma \in G \to \sigma.\text{cycleType} = \{2,1,1,1\} \to |G| \notin \{10, 20, 40\}
$$

### Plain Language

In the Lean proof of the concrete Abel-Ruffini theorem (`abel-ruffini-oq-04-oq-01`), the Galois
group of $x^5 - 4x + 2$ is determined to be $S_5$ by ruling out possible smaller group orders:
$|G| \neq 10$, $|G| \neq 20$, $|G| \neq 40$.

All three eliminations follow the **same Sylow argument**:
- The unique Sylow 5-subgroup has order 5 (cyclic, generated by a 5-cycle)
- A transposition in $G$ would normalize this Sylow subgroup
- But a transposition cannot normalize a 5-cycle subgroup → contradiction

The three lemmas `gal_card_ne_10`, `gal_card_ne_20`, `gal_card_ne_40` repeat this pattern
verbatim for each case. The question is whether these can be factored into a **single generic lemma**
parameterized by the order $k$.

### Why This Matters

This is a concrete instance of proof engineering with theory value: the abstracted lemma would be
a reusable Galois theory fact about transitive subgroups of $S_p$ with unique Sylow subgroups.
It might also be contribuable to Mathlib alongside the classical result that any transitive
subgroup of $S_p$ containing a transposition is all of $S_p$.

## Initial Thoughts

### Potential Approaches

1. **Direct generalization**: State the lemma with `k : ℕ` as a parameter, `hk : k ∈ {10, 20, 40}`,
   and prove by cases. The `by cases hk` tactic should reduce to the existing 3 proofs.
   - Why it might work: The existing proofs are already written; wrapping them is mechanical.
   - Risk: The 3 proofs may use different intermediate facts that don't unify cleanly.

2. **Abstract Sylow argument**: State a group-theoretic lemma independent of the specific order,
   using Sylow's theorem hypotheses directly. Parameterize over `p : ℕ` (prime), `k : ℕ` (group order).
   - Why it might work: The mathematical argument is uniform; Lean's `Sylow` API supports this.
   - Risk: Lean's `Sylow` API may require more abstract setup than the concrete `decide`-based proofs.

### Key Difficulties

- The existing proofs use `decide` or `native_decide` for finite group computations; the generic
  version may need to maintain computability or switch to non-computational reasoning.
- Identifying exactly which Sylow hypothesis is used (uniqueness, normality, order).

### What Would a Proof Need?

- Access to `AbelRuffiniOQ04OQ01.lean` to extract the shared proof structure
- Mathlib's `Sylow` API: `Sylow.card_eq_card_pow_orderOf`, `Sylow.subgroup_normal_of_card_eq_prime_pow`
- The concrete `sigma` (transposition) in `S₅` that witnesses membership
