# Knowledge: brianchon-theorem-oq-01-oq-01

## Problem

OQ-01 of `brianchon-theorem-oq-01`: **eliminate the shared `conic_implies_pascal`
axiom** — i.e. prove Pascal's hexagon theorem in the projective ℝ³ model used by
`BrianchonTheorem.lean` / `BrianchonTheoremAristotle.lean`. Concretely: six points
on a symmetric conic `C` make the three opposite-side intersections of the
inscribed hexagon collinear:

```
det( AB∩DE , BC∩EF , CD∩FA ) = 0      whenever the six points lie on C.
```

This is the single fact both the Brianchon and the Pascal gallery entries
axiomatize. Discharging it would make Brianchon's theorem (and Pascal's)
unconditional.

## Prior knowledge (from parent `brianchon-theorem-oq-01`)

- The conclusion is a **genuine polynomial identity**: numerical evidence
  (200k+ random hexagons; rank-1, rank-2, and nondegenerate symmetric conics;
  residuals 1e-9 … 1e-28) shows `det(P,Q,R)` lies in the ideal generated by the
  six conic equations `pᵢᵀ C pᵢ = 0`, with **no nondegeneracy hypothesis**.
- Discharge does **not** require Sylvester's law of inertia; the broken
  `PascalsHexagon.lean` Sylvester section can be avoided.
- The parent's preferred route is a **direct `linear_combination` / `polyrith`**
  ideal-membership proof, delegated to Aristotle (`BrianchonTheoremAristotle.lean`
  stages it as a single `sorry`). The cofactors are degree ≈10 polynomials in 18
  coordinates — beyond hand derivation and beyond `polyrith`'s default reach.
