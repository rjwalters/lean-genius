# de-moivre-oq-02-oq-02

**Problem**: Cross Product-to-Sum for Chebyshev Polynomials T and U

## Problem Summary

Can the T·T product-to-sum formula `2·T_m(x)·T_n(x) = T_{m+n}(x) + T_{m-n}(x)` (proved in OQ-02)
be extended to Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind U_n?

**Answer: YES** — the mixed T·U formula `2·T_m(x)·U_n(x) = U_{m+n}(x) + U_{n-m}(x)` holds in
any CommRing R. This was proved by paired integer induction on the polynomial identity, then
evaluated at cos θ for the real form.

## Key Mathematical Content

- **Polynomial identity** (T_mul_U_product): `2·T_m · U_n = U_{m+n} + U_{n-m}` in `R[X]`
- **Real form**: `2·T_m(cos θ)·U_n(cos θ) = U_{m+n}(cos θ) + U_{n-m}(cos θ)`
- **U recurrence**: `2·cos θ·U_n(cos θ) = U_{n+1}(cos θ) + U_{n-1}(cos θ)`
- **Sine spreading**: `2·cos θ·sin((n+1)θ) = sin((n+2)θ) + sin(nθ)`

## Proof Technique

Paired integer induction: invariant Q(m) = P(m) ∧ P(m-1) where P(m): 2·T_m·U_n = U_{m+n}+U_{n-m}.
Key helper: `two_X_U` proves 2·X·U_k = U_{k+1}+U_{k-1} from U_add_two with index shift.
All three induction cases (base, forward, backward) close with `linear_combination`.
