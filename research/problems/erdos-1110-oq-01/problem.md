# Problem: Erdos 1110: density of non-representables for additive bases

## Statement

### Plain Language
Erdős #1110 asks about sums of power forms p^k q^l (no summand dividing another). OQ-01 formalizes the multiplicative-monoid structure of the power forms themselves: {p^k q^l} contains 1 and is closed under multiplication, powers, and finite products — the submonoid of ℕ generated by {p,q}, the algebraic backdrop for #1110's arguments.

### Formal Statement
$$
\{p^k q^l : k,l \ge 0\} \text{ is the multiplicative submonoid of } \mathbb{N} \text{ generated by } \{p,q\}: \text{ it contains } 1 \text{ and is closed under } \cdot, \text{ powers, and finite products.}
$$
