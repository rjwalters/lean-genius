### What I did
Gave the stub OQ-01 concrete content: the multiplicative-monoid structure of the power-form set
`{p^k q^l}` (`IsPowerForm p q n := ∃ k l, n = p^k q^l`), which the parent `Erdos1110Problem.lean`
uses but never states abstractly (it has only the single-base closures
`isPowerForm_mul_base_left`/`_right`, i.e. `s ↦ s·p` and `s ↦ s·q`):
- `isPowerForm_one` — `1 = p^0 q^0` (identity).
- `isPowerForm_mul` — product of two power forms is a power form
  (`p^k q^l · p^{k'} q^{l'} = p^{k+k'} q^{l+l'}`); subsumes BOTH single-base lemmas.
- `isPowerForm_pow` — closed under powers (induction off one/mul).
- `isPowerForm_prod` — closed under finite products (`Finset.prod_induction`).
- `isPowerForm_mul_base_left'`/`_right'` — the parent's single-base lemmas re-derived as the
  `b = p` / `b = q` instances of `isPowerForm_mul`.

So `{p^k q^l}` is exactly the submonoid of `ℕ` generated by `{p, q}` — the clean algebraic backdrop
for #1110's divisibility/representation arguments.

### Lean notes

- `isPowerForm_mul`: `obtain ⟨k,l,rfl⟩` both, witness `⟨k+k', l+l', by rw [pow_add, pow_add]; ring⟩`.
- `isPowerForm_prod` via `Finset.prod_induction f (IsPowerForm p q) (fun _ _ ha hb => isPowerForm_mul ha hb) (isPowerForm_one p q) hf` (s is implicit, inferred from goal).
