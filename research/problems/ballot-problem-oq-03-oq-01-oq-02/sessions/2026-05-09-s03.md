**Mode**: ACT (RICH knowledge tier, score 209) — incremental proof of

**Outcome**: Three foundational structural lemmas added to
`BallotProblemOQ03OQ01OQ02Helpers.lean`, all sorry-free.  These package
the geometric facts that drive the **off-spine** branch of the joint
K-induction in `F_side_identity_aligned`.  They reduce the "three
moving pieces" of S57.0's analysis (K-stability, cell-shift
bookkeeping, strict-hook restructuring) by **eliminating two of them
on off-spine cells**: at any cell `x` strictly off both the row and
column of `c'`, both `hookLength` and `strictHookCells` are invariant
under `μ ↦ μ\c'`.

### Lemmas added (all `private`, all sorry-free)

#### (1) `c'_notMem_strictHookCells_of_off_spine` (line ~14562)

```lean
private lemma c'_notMem_strictHookCells_of_off_spine
    {μ : YoungDiagram} {c' : ℕ × ℕ} {x : ℕ × ℕ}
    (hx_off_row : x.1 ≠ c'.1) (hx_off_col : x.2 ≠ c'.2) :
    c' ∉ strictHookCells μ x.1 x.2
```

**Statement**: For any `x` whose row and column both differ from those
of `c'`, the corner `c'` is not in the strict hook of `x`.

**Proof** (~5 lines): unfold `strictHookCells` definition,
`rintro` the disjunction, derive contradiction from `congr_arg Prod.fst`
(or `Prod.snd`) of the membership equality.

**Why useful**: structural prerequisite for (3) below — it discharges
the precondition `c' ∉ strictHookCells μ i j` of the existing lemma
`strictHookCells_removeCorner_eq_of_not_mem`.

#### (2) `hookLength_invariant_off_spine_of_c'` (line ~14588)

```lean
private lemma hookLength_invariant_off_spine_of_c'
    {μ : YoungDiagram} {c' : ℕ × ℕ} (hc' : isCorner μ c')
    {x : ℕ × ℕ} (hxν : x ∈ removeCorner μ c' hc')
    (hx_off_row : x.1 ≠ c'.1) (hx_off_col : x.2 ≠ c'.2) :
    hookLength (removeCorner μ c' hc') x.1 x.2 =
      hookLength μ x.1 x.2
```

**Statement**: For `x ∈ μ\c'` strictly off both `c'`'s row and column,
removing `c'` leaves `hookLength` at `x` unchanged.

**Proof** (~6 lines): direct corollary of `hookLength_eq_of_not_arm_leg`
applied to corner `c'`.  The arm-of-`c'` membership condition
`x.1 = c'.1 ∧ x.2 < c'.2` fails on its first conjunct; likewise for the
leg-of-`c'` condition.

**Why useful**: at off-spine cells, the LHS integrand
`gnwProb μ c (h_μ x) x` and the RHS integrand
`gnwProb (μ\c') c (h_{μ\c'} x) x` of `F_side_identity_aligned` use the
**same** hook-length value as the K-bound, removing one of the three
moving pieces in the S57 plan note (sublemma (S1)).

#### (3) `strictHookCells_invariant_off_spine_of_c'` (line ~14616)

```lean
private lemma strictHookCells_invariant_off_spine_of_c'
    {μ : YoungDiagram} {c' : ℕ × ℕ} (hc' : isCorner μ c')
    {x : ℕ × ℕ}
    (hx_off_row : x.1 ≠ c'.1) (hx_off_col : x.2 ≠ c'.2) :
    strictHookCells (removeCorner μ c' hc') x.1 x.2 =
      strictHookCells μ x.1 x.2
```

**Statement**: For `x` strictly off both `c'`'s row and column,
removing `c'` leaves the strict hook cells of `x` unchanged.

**Proof** (~3 lines, term-mode): apply
`strictHookCells_removeCorner_eq_of_not_mem` with the `c' ∉
strictHookCells μ x` precondition supplied by lemma (1).

**Why useful**: at off-spine cells, the recursive K-step
`gnwProb _ c (K+1) x = (1/|H*(x)|) ∑_{y ∈ H*(x)} gnwProb _ c K y`
averages over the **same** strict-hook set in both `μ` and `μ\c'`,
removing the second moving piece in the S57 plan note.

### What this DOES NOT do

* **No attack on (S1) gnwProb pointwise equality.** The plan-note
  sublemma (S1) `gnwProb_invariant_off_strictHook_of_c'` posits that
  `gnwProb μ c (h_μ x) x = gnwProb (μ\c') c (h_{μ\c'} x) x` at
  off-spine cells where additionally `c ∉ strictHookCells μ x`.
  This requires **K-induction**, not just structural invariance: the
  recursive descendant `y ∈ strictHookCells μ x` may itself be
  *on*-spine of `c'`, breaking the simple-recursion argument.  The
