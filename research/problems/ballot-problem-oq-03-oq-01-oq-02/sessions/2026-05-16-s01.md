## §2  Cluster A patch (verbatim, applied to worktree)

### §2.1  New companion lemma (insert at L1853-1855)

```lean
@[simp] private lemma cast_PathMN_coe {m n₁ n₂ : ℕ} (h : n₁ = n₂) (e : PathMN m n₁) :
    ((cast (congrArg (PathMN m) h) e) : List Bool) = (e : List Bool) := by
  cases h; rfl
```

Body is identical to `cast_PathMN_val` (`cases h; rfl`); the LHS
differs only in which projection-form the simp rule matches:
* `cast_PathMN_val` (L1849, pre-existing): LHS = `(cast _ e).val`
  (the explicit `Subtype.val` projection form)
* `cast_PathMN_coe` (L1853, S78-added): LHS = `((cast _ e) : List Bool)`
  (the coercion form `↑(cast _ e)` via `Subtype.val`-as-`CoeHead`)

In Mathlib v4.26.0 elaboration, the goal residual after `simp only
[gvCanonInv, dif_neg _, dif_neg _]` on `gvCanonInv_val_other` is
displayed in `↑(cast _ _)` form rather than the `.val` form — the
S74 PARENT-TRIAGE root cause for the 4 Cluster A errors.  The `@[simp]`
tag on `cast_PathMN_coe` ensures it fires automatically when the
coercion form surfaces; explicit inclusion in the `simp only` arg
lists at L1916/L1927 is belt-and-braces for the dual-form goals that
also reference `cast_PathMN_val`'s projection-form pattern.
