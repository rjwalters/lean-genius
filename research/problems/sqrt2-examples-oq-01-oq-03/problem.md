# Problem: The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality $|\sum_i a_i b_i|^2 \leq (\sum_i a_i^2)(\sum_i ...

## Statement

### Plain Language
AVAILABLE — The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality $|\sum_i a_i b_i|^2 \leq (\sum_i a_i^2)(\sum_i b_i^2)$ also follows from a sum-of-squares argument.

### Formal Statement
For any finite families $a_1,\dots,a_n$ and $b_1,\dots,b_n$ in a linearly ordered field (or commutative ordered ring),
$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i b_i\right)^2 \le \left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^2\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^n b_i^2\right),
$$
with the sum-of-squares certificate given by Lagrange's identity
$$
\left(\sum_{i} a_i^2\right)\left(\sum_{i} b_i^2\right) - \left(\sum_{i} a_i b_i\right)^2 = \tfrac12\sum_{i,j}(a_i b_j - a_j b_i)^2 \ge 0.
$$
