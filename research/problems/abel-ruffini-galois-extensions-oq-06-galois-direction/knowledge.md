## 5-step proof plan

(Reproduced from `problem.md` §"Proof plan" for self-containment.)

1. Sylow uniqueness on H at `|H| = p · m, m < p`.
2. P normal in H (unique-Sylow ⇒ normal).
3. P generated by a p-cycle (`isCycle_of_prime_order''`, 1-LOC).
4. N_{S_p}(P) ≅ AGL(1, p).
5. H ≤ N_{S_p}(P), hence H ≤ AGL(1, p).

## Open questions / S2+ topics

- Does the Galois direction extend to *non-primitive* solvable
  subgroups of S_p? (Answer: no — non-primitive subgroups can be
  larger than AGL(1, p), e.g. wreath-product constructions.)
- Is the embedding canonical or only canonical up to conjugation in
  S_p? (Answer: up to conjugation — the choice of `P` and the
  identification of `ZMod p` with the underlying set of the action
  both have a finite ambiguity.)
- Does the dimension of `(ZMod p)ˣ` interact with the proof? (Answer:
  no — `(ZMod p)ˣ` is cyclic of order `p - 1`, which is what makes
  `AGL(1, p) = ZMod p ⋊ (ZMod p)ˣ` solvable.)
