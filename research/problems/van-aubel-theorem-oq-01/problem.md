# Problem: van Aubel's theorem: equal perpendicular diagonals of square centers

## Statement

### Plain Language
AVAILABLE: Formalize van Aubel's theorem: given an arbitrary planar quadrilateral, erect a square externally on each side and let P,Q,R,S be the four square centers; then the segments PR and QS are equal in length and perpendicular. Recommended route: model the four vertices as complex numbers a,b,c,d in C, write each square center via the 90-degree rotation i (center of square on edge from u to v is (u+v)/2 + i*(v-u)/2), and prove (R - P) = i*(S - Q) by a direct ring computation over C; this single identity yields both |PR| = |QS| and PR perpendicular to QS.
