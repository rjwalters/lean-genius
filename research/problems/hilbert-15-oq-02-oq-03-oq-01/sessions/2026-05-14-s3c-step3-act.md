## Departures from S3c-prep-7 design

1. **`Fin.card_Iic` route, not `card_filter_val_lt`.** The HEAD proof at
   `Mathlib/Data/Fintype/Fin.lean:70` uses `card_filter_val_lt`
   (line 47); both are absent at v4.26.0. PREP §3.2 observed that the
   second-direction proof can detour through
   `Finset.Iic j ⊆ Finset.univ.filter p` (by antitonicity) plus
   `Fin.card_Iic = j.val + 1` — sidestepping the helper backport
   entirely. Saved ~5 LOC and one helper.
2. **Explicit tactic chains instead of `grind`.** Mathlib HEAD uses
   `by grind` in three places (the `h1` subset proof and final `mp`
   direction). Replaced with `by_contra; push_neg; exact hk (hp x k hne hx)`
   and `by_contra hne; exact absurd hlt (Nat.not_lt.mpr (h1 j hne))`.
   PREP §2.5 nominated this as the v4.26.0-robust fallback.
3. **`omega` not `rcases (T.1 ⟨1, j⟩).val with _ | _ | _`** for the
   `Fin 2` case `val ≠ 0 → val = 1`. The PREP nominee can lose the
   discriminant when the term isn't a local variable. Direct path:
   establish `hne_val : .val ≠ 0` from `hne : T.1 ⟨1, j⟩ ≠ 0` via
   `Fin.ext` contrapositive, then `omega` with `.isLt < 2`.
4. **Explicit `Finset` type ascription** in the filter inside
   `skewSSYTFin_row1_step_function`'s if-condition matches the
   existing Part XIV style and keeps elaboration unambiguous.
