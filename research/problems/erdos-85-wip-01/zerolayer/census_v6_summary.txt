# Zero-layer used-sector census v6 — 2026-08-10

The v6 model strengthens the v5 per-used-component after-contact excess
constraint from

    excess(e) <= 2 * (k_e - 1)

to the exact identity

    excess(e) = 2 * (k_e - 1).

This equality is proved graph-theoretically by
`degree_sixteen_zeroLayer_used_component_row_after_contact_excess`
(commit `77513d8b8b`).  The reduced orders form a partition of sixteen with
every part at least two by
`degree_sixteen_zeroLayer_used_component_reduced_partition`
(commit `aa1ee79fad`).
