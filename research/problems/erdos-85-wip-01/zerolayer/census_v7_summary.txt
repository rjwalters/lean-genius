# Zero-layer used-sector census v7 — 2026-08-10

The graph theorem
`degree_sixteen_zeroLayer_used_component_row_after_contact_excess`
proves that a used component of reduced order `k = |e|/3` has total
after-contact row excess `2(k-1)`.  This sum includes its diagonal term.
For the cycle components classified by the census, `Q(e,e)=2`, so the
diagonal contributes `2`; the orphan atoms must therefore contribute
exactly

    2(k-2).

This corrects the superseded v6 experiment, which accidentally assigned the
full `2(k-1)` total to the orphan atoms and was offset by two per row.
