# The sole remaining order24 automorphism action

Let G be simple, C4-free and nine-regular on78 vertices, and suppose its full automorphism group A has order24. Independently accepted2390 proves A is isomorphic to S4. Independently accepted2376 gives nine necessary character multisets, with seven distinct orbit-size patterns.

The accepted exclusions cover all but one of those character multisets:

- 2380 excludes (3,3,4,8,12,24,24), removing one multiset.
- 2392 excludes (4,4,4,6,12,24,24) and (4,4,4,6,6,6,24,24), removing two multisets.
- 2395 excludes the whole pattern (4,6,6,6,8,24,24), removing all three fixed-count multisets with those sizes. Its scope is stronger than the earlier narrower2388 exclusion.
- 2397 excludes (4,6,8,12,12,12,24) and (4,6,6,6,8,12,12,24), removing two multisets.

Thus exactly the single2376 character multiset with sizes

 (6,6,6,12,12,12,24)

remains. In class order identity/transposition/three-cycle/double-transposition/four-cycle, its fixed counts are(78,6,0,6,6). Each of the three six-orbits has cyclic C4 stabilizer and character(6,0,0,2,2); each twelve-orbit has stabilizer generated by a transposition and character(12,2,0,0,0); the24-orbit is regular. All three-cycles act freely and there are exactly seven vertex orbits.

Every component review listed above is independently PASS. This is a complete necessary reduction for all order24 full automorphism groups, not an existence or exclusion statement for the last action. In particular the earlier informal suggestion that its fixed-set complement had48 vertices was erroneous: that complement has60 vertices. The resulting capacity equality does not exclude this last case and is not used in this assembly. No global order24 exclusion is claimed.

This assembly adds no graph computation or Lean formalization. Smaller automorphism groups and global N78/Erdős85 existence remain open.
