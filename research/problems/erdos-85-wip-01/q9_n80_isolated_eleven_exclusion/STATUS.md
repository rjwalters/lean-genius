# Isolation forces at most ten residual edges

Accepted review2293 excludes the eleven-edge equality case for the residual graph in the N80/F10 cubic-fixed involution branch. Together with accepted2287, this proves e(R)<=10 whenever a residual orbit is isolated, and hence at least five units of exceptional attached-group deficit.

The proof counts two-step walks from attached vertices into the ten residual vertices. Equality would force every high attached orbit to meet isolation. Exact adjacency/deficiency commutation then contradicts each possible four-unit deficit distribution. No fixed triangle assumption or finite equality-graph enumeration is needed.

This is a peer-reviewed paper proof, not Lean formalization. It leaves isolated cases with ten or fewer residual edges and other graph branches open. Historical search receipts are unchanged, and no full Erdős85 solution is claimed.
