# Two abstract group presentations cover the kernel-four8+16 profile

Assume the explicit kernel-four S2+4 residual8+16 profile of accepted2451 and the separately submitted cycle restriction2457. The latter is an independent-review dependency: it gives Y=2C8, at least ten noninvolutions in A, and at most five involutions. The action on regular Y is faithful. The X8 point stabilizer supplies a noncentral involution: a central one would fix all eight points, contradicting2257.

Let J be the setwise stabilizer of a cycle component of Y. Since A is transitive on Y, it swaps the two components transitively, and J has order eight and index two. J acts transitively on that component: any element sending one of its vertices to another preserves the component. Its action is regular, since A acts regularly on all Y. Thus J embeds as a regular order-eight subgroup of Aut(C8), a dihedral group of order16.

Such J is abstractly C8 or D8. Indeed if it lies in the rotation subgroup of order eight, it equals it. Otherwise its rotation intersection has order four and equals the subgroup generated by the square of a cycle rotation; adjoining its reflection-coset representative gives a dihedral group of order eight. No classification of all groups of order16 is used.

Choose an element u outside J, let theta be conjugation by u on J, and put c=u squared in J. Necessarily theta is an automorphism of J, theta squared is conjugation by c, and theta(c)=c. Every element of A has a unique form h u^e with h in J and e binary. Multiplication is determined by these data:

(h,e)(k,f)=(h theta^e(k) c^(ef), e+f mod2).

Thus enumerating every automorphism theta and every c satisfying the two conditions covers every actual A, with possible repeated isomorphism types retained.
