# q9 involution n78 abelian order eight

Accepted review2255. An abelian two-subgroup containing an order8 element is exactly the cyclic group it generates. Thus no C8 times C2 subgroup exists; every commuting element of power-of-two order belongs to that cyclic group. This is not a bound on nonabelian subgroup orders. Not a full N78 exclusion or Lean formalization.
