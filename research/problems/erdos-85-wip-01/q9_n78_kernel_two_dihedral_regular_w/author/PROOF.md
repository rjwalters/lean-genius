# All W orbits are regular for a kernel-two dihedral center image

Assume the full order16 matching normal form2419, S-orbits2+4, kernel K=<z> of order2 and dihedral center image D8. The kernel is central. Its involution z fixes S pointwise and acts freely outside S: otherwise it would fix an entire outside A-orbit of size at least8, contradicting2257.

Suppose W has an orbit of size8, with stabilizer generated by an involution t. The subgroup H stabilizing an S2 vertex is free outside S, so t is outside H and swaps S2. Since t fixes a W vertex, it fixes that vertex's unique S-neighbor; this must lie in S4. Thus the image of t is a vertex-axis reflection of the square action on S4. It fixes exactly two opposite square vertices v,x, which are matching partners in S. These are its only fixed S points.

The centralizer of t contains <t,z>, of order4, so its conjugacy class has size at most4. It is not central, since a central stabilizer would fix the whole W eight-orbit. Its class size is consequently two or four. In a transitive eight-orbit of its stabilizer class, t fixes |C_A(t)|/2 points: four in the first case and two in the second.

Every fixed W vertex attaches to v or x. Moreover, the number of fixed W vertices attached to either center, within each A-orbit, is even. Indeed z preserves that A-orbit, commutes with t, fixes each S vertex and has no fixed point outside S; it pairs the fixed W vertices within each such attachment fiber.

If t has class size two, one W eight-orbit supplies four fixed W vertices. Together with v,x these exhaust the six permitted fixed points. Distributing four among two even fibers gives either four at one center, whose fixed-graph degree is then five, or two at each, making v,x adjacent fixed-graph vertices of degree three with no common neighbor. The first is impossible in either allowed six-fixed graph. The second is impossible as well: 3K2 has no degree-three vertex, while adjacent degree-three vertices in a triangle with three pendant leaves share the third triangle vertex. Here v and x cannot have a common neighbor, since S is a matching and each W vertex has a unique S-neighbor. Thus no class-size-two involution can stabilize a W eight-orbit.

If t has class size four and stabilizes two distinct W eight-orbits of the same class, it fixes two W vertices on each. Once again there are four fixed W vertices in even attachment fibers plus v,x, giving exactly the preceding contradiction. Thus at most one W eight-orbit can use any class of size four. This paragraph does not prohibit an additional residual eight-orbit of that class.

There is at most one class of size four whose image is a vertex-axis reflection. This can be proved directly from the extension relations, without a group enumeration. Choose lifts r,s of a quarter-turn and a vertex reflection. Write r^4=z^a and srs^-1=r^-1 z^c, with binary a,c. If a=0, r squared is central, and the commutator subgroup generated by r^-2 z^c is central of order at most two. Hence every conjugacy class has size at most two. If a=1, r has order8 and z=r^4. The four elements over vertex-axis reflections are exactly r^(2i)s for i=0,1,2,3. Conjugation by r adds 2-4c to their rotation exponent modulo8. This is two or six modulo8 and acts transitively on the four even exponents. Therefore all four elements form one conjugacy class, including all possible stabilizer involutions of this image type.

Combining the preceding paragraphs, W has at most one eight-orbit. But |W|=48 and every outside orbit has size8 or16 by2419, so the number of eight-orbits in W is even. It must be zero. Hence W consists of three regular sixteen-orbits. The unique S-neighbor map and eight W-neighbors at each center then show that one W orbit attaches to S2 and the other two to S4.

This is a necessary orbit restriction for the dihedral image, not a residual-profile or full order16 exclusion. Only the displayed necessary extension relations are used; the finite extension checker2470 is not required as a completeness premise. No finite search, capped retry or Lean formalization is used.
