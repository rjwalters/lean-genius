# Complete labelled central-extension cover for the three-two-orbit kernel-two subcase

Use accepted2430. The full automorphism group A has order16, its action on S has three two-point orbits, and the kernel K has order2. Write K=<z>. This normal subgroup is central, since conjugation cannot move its unique nonidentity element. The quotient is the full labelled C2^3 of independent swaps on the three S-orbits.

Choose lifts x0,x1,x2 of the three coordinate basis vectors. Every group element has a unique normal form x0^v0 x1^v1 x2^v2 z^e, with v_i,e binary: its quotient determines v and its remaining factor determines e. Each square x_i^2 belongs to K, and all commutators belong to K, since the quotient is abelian. Thus six bits specify all rewriting rules:

x_i^2=z^a_i, and x_i x_j = z^b_ij x_j x_i for i>j.

Order the three commutator bits as (b10,b20,b21). For v,w in F2^3 define

f(v,w)=sum_i a_i v_i w_i + b10 v1 w0 + b20 v2 w0 + b21 v2 w1,

with every sum modulo2. Moving the second word's generators through the first word gives multiplication

(v,e)*(w,d)=(v+w, e+d+f(v,w)).

This derives the table of every actual A in this subcase, retaining the labels of all three S-orbits. Conversely every choice of the six bits gives an associative group law on16 elements: bilinearity gives f(u,v)+f(u+v,w)=f(v,w)+f(u,v+w), which is exactly associativity. The identity is(0,0), and (v,e) has inverse(v,e+f(v,v)). The kernel{(0,0),(0,1)} is central of order2, with quotientC2^3. Thus there are no hidden consistency conditions on these six bits.

Accepted2430 supplies an outside size-eight stabilizer generated by an involution projecting to v=(1,1,1), and proves that this involution is noncentral. Squaring either lift of111 gives z to the power a0+a1+a2+b10+b20+b21. Hence the first necessary condition is that this sum vanish.

The commutators of a lift of111 with x0,x1,x2 have exponents b10+b20, b10+b21, b20+b21 respectively. It is central precisely when all three vanish, equivalently when the three b bits are equal. Noncentrality therefore excludes b=000 and b=111. There are six remaining b triples. For each, the square condition prescribes the parity of the three a bits and permits exactly four choices. This leaves exactly24 labelled tables. No claim that the24 tables are pairwise nonisomorphic is made.

The S-action is explicit: (v,e) sends (i,c) to(i,c+v_i), independently of e. The normal form subgroup H_i is v_i=0. In all24 retained tables, t=(111,0) is a noncentral involution. Its conjugacy class is exactly{t,tz}: the quotient is abelian, the class lies in this two-element coset, and noncentrality makes both appear. Thus its centralizer has size8 and the two possible lifts give conjugate stabilizer subgroups. It suffices to take T=<t> when constructing the unique outside size-eight coset action. This covers every actual outside stabilizer action up to equivariant isomorphism; arbitrary choices of origins must still be covered by any later incidence enumeration.

check.py lists all64 six-bit choices, retains the exact24 satisfying these conditions, and materializes their group tables, inverses, S-actions and A/T coset actions. For each it checks associativity over all16^3 triples, identity/inverse laws, the kernel and quotient, the two-element conjugacy class, and centralizer size8. This original30s domain is a small group-law audit, not a graph or incidence search.

The24 models constitute a necessary complete labelled group/action cover only for the specific subcase of2430. This excludes none of these24 models as graph symmetries, does not cover other kernel sizes or S-orbit patterns, and is not a Lean formalization or a solution of Erdős85.
