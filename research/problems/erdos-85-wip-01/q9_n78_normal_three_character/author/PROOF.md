# Necessary normal-C3 order-24 permutation characters

Let G be a simple C4-free nine-regular graph on78 vertices, with full automorphism group A of order24 and normal Sylow3 subgroup P. Accepted2364 gives exactly seven vertex orbits, free P, centralizer C of order12, and fixed counts eight elements0 and fifteen nonidentity elements6. Those eight elements are exactly the elements with order divisible by3: C=P times its Sylow2 subgroup; outside C conjugation inverts P, and an element whose order is divisible by3 has an order3 power generating P, hence centralizes P.

The normal-Sylow paragraph of accepted2319 classifies this case independently of any cubic Cayley hypothesis. A=P times a Sylow2 subgroup Q as a semidirect product, since their orders are coprime and multiply to24. The five order-eight groups and all characters Q to C2 give22 labelled tables. We use precisely the first22 tables of accepted2322, including all those without cubic Cayley subsets. The four-Sylow classification and its Cayley exclusions are not used.

Since P is free, every vertex stabilizer is a 2-subgroup, and accepted2315 bounds its order by8. Every orbit is therefore A/H for such a subgroup H. The program generates all these subgroups by starting at the identity subgroup and adjoining every element of 2-power order, closing under multiplication, discarding closures larger than8. Every target subgroup has a chain of such extensions entirely within itself, so none is omitted. Finite multiplicatively closed subsets containing identity are subgroups. An early oversized closure is safely discarded since further closure only grows.

For each H the number of fixed left cosets of x is the number of a with a^-1*x*a in H, divided by |H|. The program checks divisibility and computes the full24-entry character. Identical characters are grouped, preserving all underlying subgroups. Any character exceeding the required fixed count at an element can be discarded, since contributions are nonnegative.

The remaining task enumerates all nondecreasing multisets of seven characters summing to the prescribed character (identity78, order divisible by3 zero, all others6). The DFS subtracts only componentwise-bounded vectors. Its identity-coordinate bounds use sorted orbit sizes: remaining sizes are at least the currently selected size and at most the largest retained size. These are necessary bounds only. All complete sums are saved.
