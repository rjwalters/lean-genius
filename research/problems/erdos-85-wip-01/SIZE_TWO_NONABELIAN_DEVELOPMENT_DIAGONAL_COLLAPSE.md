# Size-two nonabelian development: cyclic-or-dihedral collapse

## Scope

This note cuts one proposed countermodel class for the all-size-two
`A-REG-NONBIP` packing problem. It does **not** prove the packing
contradiction.

Suppose all component coordinates are identified with one finite group
`G` of order `2q`, and every restricted owner factor is developed by
right differences:

    (X_ce)_(x,y) = 1  iff  y x⁻¹ ∈ A_ce,

where `|A_ce| = 2` and reciprocity is represented by
`A_ec = A_ce⁻¹`. Left-development, or the convention
`x⁻¹y ∈ A_ce`, gives the same conclusion.

The self-indexed factor `X_cc` is symmetric, 2-regular, and connected.
Symmetry says `A_cc = A_cc⁻¹`. There are exactly two cases.

### Inverse-pair case

If the two elements are not involutions, they are distinct and inverse, so

    A_cc = {s, s⁻¹}

for some `s ∈ G`.

## Exact collapse

The undirected Cayley graph defined by `{s,s⁻¹}` has connected components
equal to the right cosets of the cyclic subgroup `⟨s⟩`. Therefore
connectedness of `X_cc` implies `G = ⟨s⟩`. Hence `G` is cyclic.

### Two-involution case

Otherwise `A_cc = {a,b}` for distinct involutions `a,b`. Connectedness
says `G = ⟨a,b⟩`. Put `r = ab`. Then `a r a = r⁻¹`, every word in
`a,b` is either a power of `r` or `a` times a power of `r`, and `⟨r⟩`
has index two. Thus `G` is the dihedral group generated by the two
reflections `a,b` (with the usual small degenerate cases excluded by
`|G| = 2q ≥ 16`).

Therefore a connected symmetric two-element Cayley diagonal forces the
ambient regular group to be **cyclic or dihedral**. No other nonabelian
group can occur.

Consequently a common regular group development reduces to two sharply
defined cases. The inverse-pair case is exactly the already-audited cyclic
coherent-factorization system. The only genuinely nonabelian survivor is
the two-reflection development over the dihedral group of order `2q`.
Extraspecial and all other non-dihedral 2-groups are excluded before the
complete-difference equations are used.

## Relation to the current frontier

This is stronger than observing that a particular nonabelian search has no
solution: it is a parameter-free classification of the whole
single-regular-development ansatz. It also says precisely how the q=8
cyclic coherent-factorization UNSAT result can still be evaded: only by
the two-reflection dihedral branch.

The surviving countermodel route must abandon at least one of:

1. one common regular group action on every labelled component;
2. representing the self-indexed Hamilton cycle by one inverse pair or two
   reflections;
3. simultaneous development of diagonal and off-diagonal factors.

The result does **not** exclude arbitrary noncommuting permutation
developments. Within regular group developments, it leaves the exact
dihedral two-reflection branch, which needs a faithful construction or
obstruction. It supplies a classification/collapse, not the missing
ternary packing contradiction.
