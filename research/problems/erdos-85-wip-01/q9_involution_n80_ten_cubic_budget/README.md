# Residual degree restriction at N80 with ten fixed vertices

In the branch where an involution fixes ten vertices inducing a cubic graph, the ten residual vertices cannot all have degrees two or three. At least one residual vertex must have degree 0, 1, 4, or 5. This follows from exact attached-incidence and zero-codegree identities, two small commutation/cardinality contradictions, and a separate lemma excluding a free involution on any cubic C4-free graph of order ten.

The companion lemma uses only a five-orbit binary quotient and the three possible odd loop counts; it does not rely on classifying cubic graphs. Reviews 2250 and 2251 independently accept both paper proofs. Supplementary finite checks verify the tiny quotient counts and 1,392 integer identity cases. These do not exclude all residual-degree patterns, the other ten-fixed-vertex fixed-degree branch, the entire N80 case, or Erdős 85. The proofs are not full Lean formalizations.
