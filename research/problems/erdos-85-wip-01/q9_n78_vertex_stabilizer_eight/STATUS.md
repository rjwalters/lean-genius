Every vertex stabilizer in the automorphism group of a simple C4-free nine-regular graph on78 vertices has order at most eight. The proof excludes mixed stabilizers of order12 and24 and explicitly handles the four-fixed-star case.

Independently accepted in Squad review 2315. Author and peer files are preserved byte for byte, including source paths and original capped-run receipts where present. The bank manifest pins every archived file; source manifests retain their original path semantics.

This is paper research with independently checked finite certificates where applicable, not Lean formalization. It does not solve the full Erdős85 problem or convert any UNKNOWN search into a negative result.
