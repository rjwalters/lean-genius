# Graph-side cover of the 25 historical H1 cubes

`Erdos85OneHighCube25GraphCover.lean` proves that every graph satisfying `OneHighPureFamilyCnfConstraints a R` has both an edge from vertex 4 to one of vertices 10..14 and an edge from vertex 9 to one of vertices 15..19. The existing theorem `oneHighFamilyUnmatched_not_misses_farBlock` supplies each choice: the last vertex in each five-vertex block is unmatched, has exact far degree six, and can have at most one neighbor in each of the six eligible blocks. Therefore every such graph satisfies at least one of the 25 edge pairs. This is a cover; disjointness is not claimed.

For each of the 18 archive tags with all 25 input and proof filenames present, the recorded unit pairs are exactly `{301,...,305} × {456,...,460}`. `binding.py` rechecks an actual CNF edge-allocation prefix per tag, including its hash, and establishes that those IDs are precisely the graph edges in the theorem. All 18 checked profiles are recorded in its result. The earlier 710-input audit binds each cube to the frozen base plus its recorded two units.

The two five-literal disjunctions do not appear directly as clauses of the base CNFs (the initial `check.py` experiment records this). Coverage instead follows from the graph constraints. This package does NOT prove that every arbitrary satisfying CNF assignment satisfies those constraints, replay any DRAT, claim any cube UNSAT, or mark an H1 target closed. It supplies the graph-side coverage statement and the empirical DIMACS-ID connection used by the certificate route. Proof validity and the rest of that route remain separate obligations.
