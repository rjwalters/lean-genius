# Accepted N80 D5 five-311 exclusion

Review 2511 accepts the complete exclusion of D5 with five 311 groups in the N80 cubic-fixed involution branch, composing twenty independently accepted dependency reviews. Other D5 counts, the full N80 branch and Erdős 85 remain open; no Lean theorem is claimed.

This archive preserves source payloads, original review states, independent reviewer evidence, and the review 2483 assist. Partial and capped earlier runs retain their original scope. In particular the capped 2497 search remains incomplete and is not used by the final composition; only its separately audited paper identity is needed. Original absolute paths record provenance; computational entrypoints are not claimed portable.

Files of at least one million bytes are stored with deterministic lossless gzip compression. provenance.json maps each logical path to the stored file and gives both original-content and stored hashes. verify.py checks both hashes and exact archive membership. It checks archival integrity, not mathematical correctness. No original search was rerun to prepare this archive.
