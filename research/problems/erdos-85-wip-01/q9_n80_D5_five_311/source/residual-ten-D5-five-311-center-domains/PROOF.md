# Necessary fixed-center group domains for41 D5/s5 edge cases

For s=5 and t=0, five fixed-center groups have type311 and the other five have type111. Each311 group contains exactly one of the five high involution pairs and four low vertices (two low pairs). Its high supports cover three residual involution orbits, so its two low pairs must use exactly the other two residual support orbits. Each111 group contains three low pairs with distinct residual support orbits. This follows from C4-freeness: a fixed center and a residual vertex cannot have two common attached neighbors.

The low involution pairing is the necessary normalization from2486. Enumerate every two-low-orbit choice of the required two support types for each labeled high group, and every three-low-orbit choice with distinct supports for an unlabeled111 group.

Any two vertices sharing a fixed center already have that center as a common neighbor. Therefore they cannot have a common neighbor in the current forced70-vertex graph. Every candidate group is checked against all these pairs. Its forced induced edges must also have maximum degree one, since two internal neighbors of a group vertex would give that vertex and the fixed center two common neighbors.

Every low vertex has exactly seven W-neighbors. It has at most one in any fixed-center group, and none in the three groups whose centers are adjacent to its own center: such an edge would form a C4 through the two centers. There are seven remaining groups, including its own. Thus it has exactly one internal-group W-neighbor. High vertices need not have an internal neighbor. Given a candidate group's forced internal matching, a complete small matching recursion checks whether all unmatched lows can be covered using possible internal edges, allowing unmatched highs to remain unused. This is necessary only; relaxing involution invariance of the chosen internal matching cannot remove an actual group.
