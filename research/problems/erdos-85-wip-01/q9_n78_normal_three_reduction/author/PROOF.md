# Normal Sylow3 order24 reduces to S3 times V4

Let G be simple, C4-free and nine-regular on78 vertices, with full automorphism group A of order24 and a normal Sylow3 subgroup. Accepted2368 gives a complete necessary action-character cover: the only retained labelled groups are seven nonzero E8-character semidirect products and D8:01010101. The E8 labels have orbit sizes (3,3,12,12,12,12,24) or (6,6,6,12,12,12,24); the D8 label has (3,3,12,12,12,12,24) or (6,12,12,12,12,12,12).

Accepted2372 independently verifies the COMPLETE first quotient case, excluding (3,3,12,12,12,12,24). Its second completed case gives264 necessary quotients for (6,6,6,12,12,12,24), but that list is not needed for this assembly. The third quotient case remains UNKNOWN and provides no negative evidence.

Accepted2371 independently excludes (6,12,12,12,12,12,12) by a paper argument. Its proof does not depend on the capped third quotient case. Consequently both D8-character alternatives are excluded. Only the nonzero E8-character family survives, with sizes (6,6,6,12,12,12,24).

For each nonzero homomorphism E8 to C2, its kernel is V4 and is central in the semidirect product. Choosing a complementary involution splits E8 as V4 times C2; that complementary C2 inverts the normal C3. Thus all seven labelled survivors are isomorphic to S3 times V4.

Accepted2374 supplies the full necessary geometry in this remaining case: the three central involutions have disjoint six-vertex fixed sets, each a matching and each an A orbit; there are no edges between these sets. The remaining orbits are one regular U24 and three R12 orbits labelled by pairs of the fixed sets. U attaches once to each fixed set, while each R orbit attaches once to each member of its labelled pair. G[U] is a cubic Cayley graph, G[R] is five-regular, each R vertex has two U neighbors, and each U vertex has one neighbor in each R orbit. The zero-codegree graph restricts to three disjoint K6s on the18 fixed-set vertices and has no edges from those vertices to the complement.

All four component reviews are independently PASS. This assembly removes the D8 family and narrows every normal-Sylow order24 candidate to the stated S3 times V4 geometry. It does not exclude that remaining geometry or any nonnormal-Sylow group, smaller automorphism group, or global graph existence. In particular it does not turn UNKNOWN into a complete result. No new computation or Lean formalization is claimed.
