# Independent elementary abelian rank bound at N78

Accepted review2258 excludes a subgroup C2^5 using the six-fixed-vertex bound and stabilizer intersections. Thus elementary abelian two-rank is at most4. The later, stronger accepted2262 bound of16 on every two-subgroup implies the same conclusion; this archive preserves the independent earlier proof. It is not a separate improvement over2262 or a full graph exclusion, and it is not formalized in Lean.
