# Power-of-two automorphism orders at N78

Accepted review2253 excludes automorphisms whose orders are divisible by16. An order8 automorphism has an involution fourth power fixing exactly six vertices inducing three disjoint edges; an order4 automorphism has an involution square fixing either two vertices forming an edge or six forming three disjoint edges. The triangle-with-leaves fixed graph cannot occur for these involution powers. This does not bound the order of an arbitrary two-subgroup or exclude the remaining matching cases. Paper group-action proof, not Lean.
