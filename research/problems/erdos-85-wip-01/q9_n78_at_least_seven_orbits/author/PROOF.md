# Every N78 candidate has at least seven automorphism orbits

Let G be a simple C4-free nine-regular graph on78 vertices and let A=Aut(G). We compose five independently accepted packets to prove that A has at least seven vertex orbits. All five review records were freshly PASS, and every source manifest and its payload was rehashed at assembly time. The exact records and hashes are saved alongside this proof.

Accepted2353 proves at least six orbits. It combines the earlier fewer-than-five exclusion with the complete five-orbit split-center, double-attachment and single-attachment exclusions. Its independent audit specifically discharged historical input-completeness conditions in2347/2348 through accepted2345. No part of that assembly remains conditional.

Suppose there are exactly six orbits. Accepted2346 exhausts all possible orbit-size partitions and necessary degree quotients, leaving only(6,6,6,12,24,24) and(6,12,12,12,12,24), at group orders24 or48. Its finite output is COMPLETE with no UNKNOWN/unvisited case, and its paper eliminations were independently checked.

Accepted2349 excludes group order48 and further reduces the order24 case:24 quotients of the first size pattern and48 of the second remain. In particular all six-orbit vertex stabilizers at size6 are Klein-four. The old conditional reference to2346 in that frozen packet is discharged by2346's PASS review.

Accepted2351 excludes the first size pattern. Each surviving matrix has a six-vertex orbit of internal degree2. Its group action is faithful by the involution fixed-graph classification. Neither C6 nor2K3 permits the resulting transitive faithful order24 action; in the latter case a normal order3 subgroup would force the action into a normalizer of order36. Thus none of the24 first-pattern quotients can occur. Accepted2350 gives a separate alternative proof, but is not required in this five-premise composition.

Accepted2354 excludes the second size pattern. The matching six-orbit has attached fibers2+2+4. At matched centers, the common Klein-four stabilizer has two involutions fixing attached neighbors at each endpoint; those two-element subsets of its three involutions intersect. The resulting exactsix-fixed graph has adjacent cubic centers with no common neighbor, contradicting the accepted fixed-graph classification. Every one of the48 remaining matrices was checked to have the requisite structure.

Therefore exactly six orbits is impossible. Together with2353, A has at least seven vertex orbits.

This is an automorphism-orbit lower bound, not a graph nonexistence theorem. In particular asymmetric candidates and all graphs with seven or more automorphism orbits are not excluded. The N78 existence question, N80 and the full Erdős85 objective remain unresolved. This assembly runs no new search and makes no Lean-formalization claim.
