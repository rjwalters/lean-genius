# Count and counter composition

The full prefix count is expressed as the finite sum of Boolean indicators; the supplied equivalence preserves that sum, and the product sum becomes the six block cardinalities. Pair clauses prohibit distinct true items in a block. Each arbitrary-auxiliary reverse counter on negated inputs bounds false inputs at 24, hence true inputs at least six. The occupancy theorem therefore gives the designated Cartesian pair. No extra graph or canonical witness hypothesis is smuggled into the result.

Concrete CNF schema satisfaction, bijection/target identification and cube UNSAT remain separate. This is a conditional semantic cover, not a candidate exclusion.
