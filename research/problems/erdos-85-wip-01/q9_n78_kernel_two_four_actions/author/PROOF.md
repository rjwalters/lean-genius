# Four labeled actions remain in the kernel-two center case

Assume the full order16 matching normal form2419 with S-orbits2+4 and center-action kernel of order2. The image cover2450, together with2453 and2466, leaves the three dihedral graph images. Use2470's exhaustive eight extension laws with bits(a,b,c), and its three nontrivial characters(u,v) on S2. Accepted2472 makes W three regular16-orbits. The full vertex-reflection exclusion2475 is an explicit independent-review dependency of this composition.

First, any residual8+16 profile requires at least ten noninvolutions in A. Write R=X8 union Y16. As in2425, cubic residual degree and the eight-vertex cubic obstruction give X internal degree one, X-to-Y degree two and Y internal degree two. For the three regular W orbits, let b_i be their Y-degrees. They lie between zero and three and sum to six. If all are two, every outside vertex has two Y-neighbors, so a Y vertex has at least six zero-codegree partners among the other Y vertices, contrary to the global degree five. Otherwise sum binomial(b_i,2)>=4. The free-middle lemma2438 gives at least four free unordered-Y-pair orbits from W and another from Y. Such orbits correspond to inverse pairs of noninvolutions of A. Hence there are at least ten noninvolutions.

Second, if a=0 then residual8+8+8 is impossible. The necessary lift relations show that r squared is central, and the commutator subgroup is generated by r^-2 z^c, of order at most two. Consequently every conjugacy class has size at most two. Any residual eight-orbit stabilizer is noncentral, so its class has size two and it fixes four vertices in that orbit. The three residual orbits must use distinct stabilizer classes or an involution would fix at least eight points. Stabilizers therefore act freely on the other residual orbits, making all cross-degrees even. Each internal degree is odd and cannot be three by the cubic-eight obstruction, so it is one. A symmetric quotient with three rows, diagonal one and off-diagonal even entries summing to two would require a degree-one graph on three indices, impossible.

The following arithmetic is read directly from the complete group tables2470, and is also immediate by squaring the normal forms. In lexicographic bit order000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111 the numbers of noninvolutions are4,8,12,8,6,10,14,10. The admissible character lists, after requiring an involution outside H with its fixed S points plus its own eight-orbit fixed points at most six, are:

    000: (0,1), (1,0), (1,1)
    001: (0,1), (1,1)
    010: none
    011: (0,1), (1,0)
    100: (0,1), (1,0), (1,1)
    101: (0,1), (1,1)
    110: none
    111: (0,1), (1,0)

An admissible outside eight-orbit is necessary because the72 outside vertices cannot be partitioned solely into sixteen-orbits. All a=0 laws are now excluded: their8+8+8 profile fails the parity argument; their8+16 profile either has fewer than ten noninvolutions or, for010, has no admissible stabilizer at all. Law110 likewise has no admissible stabilizer. Law101 is excluded by2475.

Law100 is the dihedral group of order16 and has only six noninvolutions, so it must have residual8+8+8. Its character(1,1) allows only the vertex-reflection stabilizer class. A generator in this class fixes two S vertices and two points on each of the three residual eight-orbits, totaling eight. Thus this character is excluded. The other two characters are not excluded by this argument.

The remaining upper cover has exactly four labeled actions:

* Law100 with character(0,1), residual8+8+8 only.
* Law100 with character(1,0), residual8+8+8 only.
* Law111 with character(0,1), either residual profile still allowed.
* Law111 with character(1,0), either residual profile still allowed.

For law111 the presentation has r of order8, s squared equal to r^4 and srs^-1=r^3. Replacing s by rs gives an involution, so the abstract group is the semidihedral group of order16; its noncentral involutions project to edge-axis reflections in the fixed S4 labeling. The label distinction from law101 matters and is retained.

This is a necessary upper cover of kernel-two actions only. It does not assert that any of the four can occur, and it does not address the kernel-four cases or exclude full order16. The numerical table extraction uses an already complete accepted group domain, not a new graph search or a resumed capped enumeration. No global graph or Lean claim is made. Independent review is requested after2475.
