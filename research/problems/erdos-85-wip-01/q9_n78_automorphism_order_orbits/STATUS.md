# Accepted review 2264

For a hypothetical C4-free graph on 78 vertices with minimum degree nine, the automorphism group has order dividing 48. It has at least three vertex orbits. If it has exactly three, the group has order 48, the orbits have sizes 6,48,24, and their adjacency quotient is [[1,8,0],[1,5,3],[0,6,3]]. The six-vertex orbit is a matching. These necessary conditions do not exclude the three-orbit case or assert a common pointwise-fixing involution. This is a peer-reviewed paper proof, not a Lean formalization.
