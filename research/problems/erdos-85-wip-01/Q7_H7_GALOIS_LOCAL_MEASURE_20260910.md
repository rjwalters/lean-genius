## Conjugate weights

Every irreducible factor has form f=x²+bx+c, discriminant d, roots
lambda_plus/minus=(-b plus/minus sqrt(d))/2. Use rational numbers u,v and
weights

    w_plus=(u+v/sqrt(d))/2,
    w_minus=(u-v/sqrt(d))/2.

These weights are exchanged by field conjugation. For a rational r with
0<r²<d, choose nonnegative rational cone coordinates p,n and set
u=p+n, v=r(p-n). Both conjugate weights are strictly positive whenever
u>0; they vanish together when u=0.

Let Q_k=lambda_plus^k+lambda_minus^k and
E_k=(lambda_plus^k-lambda_minus^k)/sqrt(d). Both are integers generated by
the factor recurrence, from (Q0,Q1)=(2,-b), (E0,E1)=(0,1).
The pair's moment is (uQ_k+vE_k)/2. Thus all equations and the retained
verification use rational arithmetic, with no numerical algebraic roots.
Global cone-coordinate totals are each the factor multiplicity, making
global u=2*multiplicity and v=0. The resulting eigenvalue multiplicities
are exactly those of the reviewed psi7.
