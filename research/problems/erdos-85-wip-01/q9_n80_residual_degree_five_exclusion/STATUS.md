# Residual degree five excluded in the N80/F10 cubic-fixed branch

Accepted review 2285, with two independent paper audits, excludes every residual-degree-five shape in a hypothetical simple C4-free nine-regular graph on 80 vertices whose involution fixes a cubic graph on ten vertices.

The proof sums the four leaf columns of adjacency/deficiency commutation. The resulting deficit inequality excludes matching central markings for all three residual shapes and the star marking in two shapes. In the final star case, the center row forces ordinary outer groups; full cross matchings then force an internal edge that completes a C4.

The argument uses accepted structural reviews 2259, 2272, 2274 and 2277. It requires no representative enumeration or capped-search outcome, and supersedes the finite degree-five subcase exclusions mathematically. Historical capped receipts and their UNKNOWN/UNVISITED statuses are unchanged. In conjunction with accepted 2251, the remaining cubic-fixed branch must have a residual vertex of degree zero, one or four.

This is a paper proof, not Lean formalization. Other residual-degree and fixed-graph branches remain open; this does not solve Erdős 85.
