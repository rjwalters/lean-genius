Choose coordinates g in Z/3 on each free orbit, with tau(g)=g+1. Each invariant perfect matching is specified by one offset s: an edge from g in one orbit to g+s in the other. Independently changing origins in the six orbits changes offsets by vertex-potential differences. On every path component all offsets can therefore be set to0. On a4-cycle, a spanning path can be normalized and the one remaining offset is its signed cycle sum v.

If v=0, the lifted4-cycle consists of three4-cycles and is forbidden. If v is1 or2, traversing the base cycle changes g by a generator of Z/3, so its lift is a single12-cycle. These are the only possible C4 obstructions in the induced graph on the18 attached vertices and two fixed vertices: among attached vertices, the components are path lifts or that cycle lift. A C4 through one fixed vertex would require two of its attached vertices to have a second common neighbour; the induced matching and crossmatching each give at most one neighbour in that attached set. A C4 through both fixed vertices would require a common neighbour, but their attached sets are disjoint.

Thus for each rooted partial/full matching, forest offsets have one normalized choice; cyclic offsets have two nonzero choices for a specified generator tau. Simultaneously replacing all coordinates g by-g exchanges those two choices while replacing tau by its inverse, so they can be identified only if generator inversion is allowed. No such identification is required for the stated two-choice cover.
