# Necessary asymmetric attachment profiles for S2+4 with kernel four

Assume the N78 simple C4-free9-regular graph has full automorphism group A of order16, with S-orbits S2 of size two and S4 of size four in the matching normal form2419. Let K be the kernel of the S action, and suppose |K|=4. This is one of the remaining cases after accepted2447. Let H be the order-eight point stabilizer of S2. Accepted2419 makes H free outside S.

## The quotient and regular W orbits

The faithful S-action quotient B=A/K has order four and acts transitively on S4. Hence that four-point action is regular and its point stabilizers in A are exactly K. The S2 action is a nontrivial character of B, with kernel H/K of order two.

Every outside A-orbit has size eight or sixteen by2419. Since the outside set has72 vertices, at least one eight-orbit exists. Its point stabilizer is generated by an involution t outside H, because H is free there. Its image in B is therefore a nonidentity involution outside H/K. If B were cyclic of order four, its only nonidentity involution would lie in its unique subgroup of order two, namely H/K. Contradiction. Consequently B is the Klein four group.

If w belongs to W, its stabilizer fixes its unique S-neighbor. Over S2 this stabilizer lies in H; over S4 it lies in K, which is a subgroup of H. Freeness of H outside S makes every W stabilizer trivial. Thus W is three regular sixteen-point orbits. Denote by W0 the single orbit attached to S2, and by W1,W2 the two attached to S4. These counts follow from the unique-neighbor map: a regular16-orbit contributes eight neighbors at each S2 point or four at each S4 point, while every S point requires eight.

Each r in R has exactly one W-neighbor in each of the six groups B_s: the six length-two S endpoints through its W-neighbors must be distinct by C4-freeness. Hence every r has two neighbors in W0 and a total of four in W1 union W2. Every W vertex has exactly three R-neighbors by2419.

## Residual profile8+16

Suppose R=X8 union Y16. The stabilizer parity and cubic-eight argument of2425 force X internal degree one, X-to-Y degree two, Y-to-X degree one, and Y internal degree two. This deduction uses only the cubic residual graph and the regularity of Y, not the faithful S-action or any group exponent.

For a vertex in X, its order-two stabilizer acts freely on each regular W_i. Thus its W1/W2 neighbor counts are even, sum to four, and are one of(0,4),(2,2),(4,0). Balance between X8 and each W16 gives W1/W2-to-X counts(0,2),(1,1),(2,0), respectively. Subtracting these from total R-degree three yields W1/W2-to-Y counts(3,1),(2,2),(1,3). W0 has X-degree one and Y-degree two by the same balance.

The middle possibility is impossible. It would give every W vertex two Y-neighbors; every X and Y vertex also has two Y-neighbors. As Y has no S-neighbors, a vertex y in Y would have only nine nonreturning two-step endpoints in the fifteen other Y vertices, leaving at least six zero-codegree partners, whereas the global total is five. This is the same endpoint argument of2441, with its hypotheses established directly here.

After swapping W1 and W2 if necessary, the only remaining profile is:

* W0-to-(X,Y)=(1,2);
* W1-to-(X,Y)=(2,1);
* W2-to-(X,Y)=(0,3).

Reverse degrees are X-to-(W0,W1,W2)=(2,4,0) and Y-to-(W0,W1,W2)=(2,1,3). These are necessary degree profiles, not realizations.

## Residual profile8+8+8

Suppose instead R=R0 union R1 union R2 with all three orbits of size eight. Balance gives W0 exactly one neighbor in each R_i. The order-two stabilizer at a residual vertex again makes its degree into each regular W orbit even. Write a_i for the W1-to-R_i degree. Since the reverse R_i-to-W1 degree is2a_i and the combined reverse degree into W1,W2 is four, we have a_i in{0,1,2}, W2-to-R_i degree2-a_i, and sum_i a_i=3. Thus a is either(1,1,1) or a permutation of(2,1,0).

The uniform possibility(1,1,1) is impossible by the residual quotient and cross-pair argument in accepted2447. For clarity, all hypotheses of that downstream argument hold independently of its initial S2^3 assumption: R is cubic on three transitive8-orbits, W comprises three regular16-orbits, every W vertex has one neighbor in each R_i, and S has no R-neighbors. The zero-codegree bound forces the residual degree matrix to be [[0,2,1],[2,1,0],[1,0,2]] up to relabelling. Its internal degree-two orbit is C8 with faithful A=Aut(C8), and the matching identifies two residual actions. Three regular W images exhaust their48 free cross pairs, while the cycle-middle incidences force16 further disjoint free pairs, contradiction. The argument uses no matching-edge identification or orbit sizes inside S after these listed hypotheses.

Consequently, after relabelling the R orbits, the only remaining W-to-R profiles are W0:(1,1,1), W1:(2,1,0), W2:(0,1,2).

This reduces the S2+4/kernel-four case to two nonuniform attachment profiles, with quotient A/K=V4. It does not exclude those profiles, the kernel-two S2+4 case, or full graph existence. No finite search or Lean formalization is used. Independent review is requested.
