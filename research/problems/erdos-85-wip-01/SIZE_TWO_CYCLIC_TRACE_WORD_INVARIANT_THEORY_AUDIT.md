## Why this literature is relevant

For the base blocks `A_tu`, reciprocity implies reversal of every colored
closed-word trace:

```text
tr(A_t0t1 A_t1t2 ... A_t(k-1)t0)
  = tr(A_t0t(k-1) ... A_t2t1 A_t1t0).
```

The q8 probe shows that reversal identities in degrees three and four,
together but not separately, already contradict the directed full-cap
empty-fibre system.  This resembles the classical invariant theory of
matrix tuples, where traces of noncommutative words generate invariants for
simultaneous conjugation.

The main primary references are:

- C. Procesi, *The invariant theory of n x n matrices*, Advances in
  Mathematics 19 (1976), 306--381,
  DOI `10.1016/0001-8708(76)90027-X`;
- S. Friedland, *Simultaneous similarity of matrices*, Advances in
  Mathematics 50 (1983), 189--265,
  DOI `10.1016/0001-8708(83)90044-0`; and
- H. Radjavi, *Simultaneous unitary invariants for sets of matrices*,
  Canadian Journal of Mathematics 20 (1968), 1012--1018,
  DOI `10.4153/CJM-1968-098-4`.

Procesi's trace algebra and its refinements say, in characteristic zero,
that simultaneous-conjugation invariants of a tuple are generated by traces
of words.  Friedland studies the corresponding simultaneous-similarity
problem.  Specht/Radjavi-type unitary criteria use traces of words involving
matrices and adjoints.

## What the named theorems would actually reconstruct

Let `M` denote a tuple of matrices and `M^T` its transposed/reversed tuple.
Equality of the appropriate trace-word invariants can, under the standard
closed-orbit/semisimplicity hypotheses, show that `M` and `M^T` are in the
same simultaneous-conjugacy orbit.  In schematic form it yields a matrix
`S` with

```text
S M_i S^(-1) = M_i^T
```

for all tuple entries, not the entrywise identities `A_tu=A_ut^T` in the
fixed cyclic base labeling.

That distinction is fatal for a direct application here.  An arbitrary
conjugating matrix:

1. need not be a permutation matrix;
2. need not preserve zero-one entries;
3. need not preserve the base-coordinate row and column projections;
4. need not fix the selected empty fibre or its diagonal block; and
5. need not preserve coefficientwise common-target caps.

Thus even a successful simultaneous-similarity reconstruction would change
the combinatorial object on which the packing inequalities are stated.

## Degrees three and four are not a general reconstruction theorem

The classical trace algebra requires traces of a generating collection of
words whose necessary length depends on matrix dimension.  It does not say
that degrees three and four determine an arbitrary q-by-q tuple, uniformly
in q.  Low-degree equality can hold for non-similar tuples; the q8 result is
a consequence of the additional zero-one, two-hole, cap, and empty-block
constraints.

The probe confirms this directly:

- all degree-three reversals alone are SAT;
- all degree-four reversals alone are SAT;
- both degrees are SAT if the empty block is removed; and
- with the empty block, restricting the trace words to any five of the six
  allowed q8 colors is SAT.

So the separator is not generic matrix reconstruction.  It is a special
mixed-degree inequality using every intermediate fibre color.

## Orthogonal invariant theory is also too strong and misaligned

Invariant theory for simultaneous orthogonal conjugation naturally includes
traces of words in both the matrices and their formal transposes.  Supplying
those transposes as independent letters would encode much more information
than the tested `T3/T4` reversal equalities.  Conversely, proving equality of
only the closed-word traces does not identify each directed block with the
transpose of its reverse block.

The desired proof should therefore not invoke a black-box assertion that
trace reversal "reconstructs reciprocity."  The q8 directed models satisfying
one trace degree are explicit countercontrols to such a shortcut.

## Useful lesson and precise surviving target

The literature supplies the correct organizing language but no terminal
theorem.  Treat the colored traces as generators in a constrained trace
algebra, and seek an identity in the ideal generated by:

```text
two affine projection equations,
zero-one/full-cap quadratic inequalities,
one zero diagonal block,
degree-3 reversal defects,
degree-4 reversal defects.
```

The computational result asks for a special Positivstellensatz/SOS-style
certificate showing that the two families of reversal defects cannot both
vanish.  Such a certificate would use the extra combinatorial relations and
could plausibly lift to arbitrary base blocks.  Generic Procesi/Friedland
simultaneous-similarity theorems cannot substitute for it.

## Verdict

The direct invariant-theory shortcut is **cut**: all trace words reconstruct
at most a conjugacy class under additional hypotheses, while the proof needs
fixed-label entrywise transpose or a contradiction compatible with the cap.
Degrees three and four have no uniform generic sufficiency theorem.

The valuable transfer is conceptual and concrete: formulate the next search
as a mixed trace-ideal/SOS certificate, not as another scalar moment or an
attempt to infer exact reciprocity from finitely many low-degree traces.
