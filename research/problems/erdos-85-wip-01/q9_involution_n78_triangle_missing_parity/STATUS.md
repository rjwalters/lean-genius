# Odd missing-pair permutation in the N78 triangle case

Accepted review2247 proves that the composition of the three missing incidence bijections must be odd. After normalizing two bijections, this excludes24 of48 permutations commuting with the free involution. The proof compares parity sums in three projections of the residual triple system. This is a partial obstruction, not a full triangle-case or N78 exclusion, and is not formalized in Lean.
