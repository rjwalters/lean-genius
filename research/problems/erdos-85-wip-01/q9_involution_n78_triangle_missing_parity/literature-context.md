# Literature cross-check for the full-Latin special case

Source: [Cycle structure of autotopisms of quasigroups and Latin squares](https://www.cs.du.edu/~petr/data/papers/cycle_structure_of_autotopisms_of_quasigroups_and_latin_squares.pdf), Theorem5.2, PDF page15 (printed page15), accessed2026-09-11.

The theorem states that a fixed-point-free permutation with m cycles of common length d is a Latin-square automorphism precisely when d is odd or m is even. Setting d=2,m=3 confirms the full-Latin order6 obstruction. This is a literature cross-check only; review2247's missing-bijection sign lemma is proved independently for the partial triple system and does not depend on this external theorem. No claim of novelty is made for the full-Latin obstruction.
