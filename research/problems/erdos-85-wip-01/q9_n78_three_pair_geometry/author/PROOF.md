# Necessary geometry for every three-two-orbit S-action at order16

Assume the N78 simple C4-free9-regular graph has full automorphism group A of order16 and the six-point matching locus S of accepted2419 consists of three A-orbits S0,S1,S2 of size two. Write H_i for the point stabilizer on S_i and K for the kernel of the action on S. No kernel size is assumed initially.

Every H_i has order eight and index two. Repeating the proof of2419 for this choice of point stabilizer gives a six-point nonfree locus S_i'. For every s in S, its A-stabilizer has order eight, hence intersects H_i in order at least four. Consequently S is contained in S_i' and equality follows by size. Thus every H_i acts freely outside S. This is the same universally applicable argument checked in2430 and does not require its kernel-two hypothesis.

A vertex w in W has exactly one S-neighbor s. Any automorphism fixing w fixes s, so A_w is contained in the corresponding H_i. But H_i is free outside S, so A_w is trivial. Therefore W48 is three regular16-orbits. Each maps equivariantly by the unique-neighbor map onto one S_i and contributes eight W-neighbors to each of its two vertices. Since every s requires exactly eight W-neighbors, there is exactly one W16 orbit over each S_i. This does not assume that the matching edges of S are the three S_i: equal two-point characters could allow crossed matching edges.

Every r in R has six W-neighbors. Their associated S-neighbors must all differ, or two paths from r to the same S vertex would form a C4. Therefore r has one neighbor in each of the six groups B_s and two neighbors in each W16 orbit.

The residual set R24 has orbit decomposition8+16 or8+8+8 by2419. Suppose it is X8 plus regularY16. R is cubic. The order-two stabilizer of x in X acts freely on Y, so its Y degree is even, zero or two. Zero would make X internally cubic, impossible by the elementary cubic-eight lemma proved in2425. Thus X-to-Y degree is two, Y-to-X degree one by balance, and internalY degree two. Since every Y vertex has two neighbors in each W16, balance between equal16-orbits also gives every W vertex exactly two Y-neighbors. Hence every vertex outside S has exactly two Y-neighbors.

Fix y in Y. Its nine neighbors lie outside S and each gives precisely one nonreturning length-two endpoint in Y other than y. At most nine of the fifteen other Y vertices have a common neighbor with y; at least six have none. However C4-freeness makes the72 nonreturning two-step endpoints from any vertex distinct, leaving exactly77-72=5 vertices without a common neighbor globally. Contradiction, as in the accepted2441 argument. We conclude that R is three transitive eight-point orbits. Every such orbit has order-two point stabilizers, and its vertices have two neighbors in each W16; balance gives every W vertex exactly one neighbor in each R8 orbit.

The S-action quotient A/K is a subgroup of C2^3 with nonzero coordinate characters, since all three S-orbits have size two. For any r in R, its stabilizer involution t lies outside every H_i, and so acts as111 on S. Thus111 belongs to the quotient image. If |K|=4, that image is a two-dimensional subspace. Its three coordinate characters cannot all be distinct: the three nonzero characters of C2^2 sum to zero, whereas their value sum on111 is one. They also cannot all agree, since the image has rank two. Exactly two characters agree and the third is independent.

Still assuming |K|=4, every possible outside stabilizer involution lies in the same four-element coset tK. None is central, since a central element fixing a point of an eight-orbit fixes its entire orbit, exceeding the six-fixed bound2257. The quotient is abelian, so each conjugacy class lies in this coset and has size two or four. If a class has size c, its involution fixes |C_A(t)|/2=8/c points on each R8 orbit whose stabilizers belong to that class. Size-two classes therefore support at most one such orbit each; a four-element coset can contain at most two size-two classes, insufficient for all three R8 orbits. Hence the coset is one conjugacy class of size four. All three R8 actions are equivariantly isomorphic, and each of the four stabilizer involutions fixes exactly two points in each.

The universal W16^3/R8^3 geometry is a necessary restriction for all three-S2 actions. The last character/conjugacy refinement applies only at kernel size four. This packet alone asserts no full exclusion. No finite search or Lean formalization is used. Independent review is requested before use as an accepted premise.
