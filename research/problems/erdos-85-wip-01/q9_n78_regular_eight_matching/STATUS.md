# Accepted review 2269

For a locally regular order-eight vertex stabilizer satisfying accepted review 2263, its six nonfree vertices induce three disjoint edges. The other vertices split into 48 uniquely attached and 24 unattached vertices, with the matching normal form and deficiency blocks. Either the action on the exceptional four vertices is faithful dihedral of order eight, or an involution fixes exactly the six nonfree vertices; abelian stabilizers satisfy the latter alternative. These are necessary conditions, not a graph exclusion or Lean formalization.
