# Further necessary attachment restrictions (unreviewed)

Assume the conclusion of submitted review 2265. Let a,b be the two special type-211 centers, the endpoints of H[M]=P6. All other groups are type 111. Each vertex in these ordinary groups saturates its internal and every allowed cross-group slot. Therefore every allowed cross block is full except possibly B_a--B_b. This block is allowed because a,b are not adjacent in H.

Each special group has four residual-degree-one vertices T and one residual-degree-two involution orbit S. Its vertices have one fixed neighbor, at most one internal neighbor, and at most one in each of six allowed cross groups. Every vertex of T fills all seven slots. A vertex of S leaves exactly one slot empty. Equivariance makes the two vertices of S have the same choice: their internal slot or their slot to the other special group.

If either special group leaves cross slots empty, the cross block has four edges, so the other special group does too. Otherwise the cross block is a full six-edge matching. Consequently there are precisely two necessary deficit regimes:

1. Both special groups have their internal matching on T only (two internal edges each), their S vertices internally unmatched, and their mutual cross matching full.
2. Both special groups have internal perfect matchings; their mutual cross block has four edges and omits exactly S on each side.

This dichotomy concerns empty slots only; it asserts neither regime can be completed.

Each special B group covers all eight noncentral residual vertices and misses the central pair. Its degree-two attached vertex u must have one neighbor in L and one in L': two neighbors in the same side would already share a central neighbor, producing a C4 with u. The two chosen neighbors cannot be an involution pair, since then u and tau(u) would share both. Thus the degree-two orbit meets two distinct leaf orbits, with one neighbor on each side at each attached vertex. The other two attached orbits have residual degree one and cover the other two leaf orbits.

All claims here are elementary deductions awaiting independent review. They do not exclude the remaining nine marked fixed graphs or produce a full graph.
