## minor

- **§3.2 [L120] — "the $C_4$-free edge bound gives $h\le 9$".** The standard bound $\mathrm{ex}(n,C_4)\le \tfrac{n}{4}(1+\sqrt{4n-3})$ gives at most 182 edges at $n=49$, hence only $h\le 21$ from $(343+h)/2\le 182$. The $h\le 9$ inequality is a Lean theorem per `STRATA_AND_SMALL_ORDERS_20260928.md`, but the paper attributes it to an unnamed bound. Either name the counting argument actually used or write "and a counting bound proved in Lean gives $h\le 9$".
- **Abstract [L54] — "it selects $r(42)=49$ in Boza's open entry".** The antecedent is the drop at the computational-evidence level; §1 says more carefully "the nonexistence hypothesis selects $r(42)=49$". In the abstract, "corresponds to selecting" or "would select" keeps the Ramsey-side statement at the same evidence level as the $f$-side statement.
- **§1 Result A [L71] — "about 1,400 solver inputs (1,412 preparation receipts over four cloud passes)".** Per `CENSUS_TIMING_20260928.md` the 1,412 receipts are re-preparations of the 1,137 cloud rows across passes (1,132 + 261 + 17 + 2), not 1,400 distinct instances; the distinct rows are 1,161. Write "1,412 input preparations for the 1,161 rows over four cloud passes" and keep "about 1,400" only if it refers to preparations.
- **§2 [L87] — "that comparison is left to a literature pass with search enabled".** `related-work` A process note in the published body; an outside reader does not know what a literature pass with search enabled is.
