## blocker

- **Preamble [L9] — literal backslashes in every rendered Lean statement.** `\renewcommand{\texttt}[1]{\path{#1}}` feeds pandoc's already-escaped `\_`, `\ `, `\#` into `\path`, which prints them verbatim. The rendered abstract reads `f(48)\=\8 and f(49)\=\7` and `BinarySquareRegularExclusion\→\¬\Erdos85Question`; §1.2 renders the hypothesis as `hno49\:\¬\C4FreeMinDegreeWitness\49\7`; 96 `\_` and 23 `\=`/`\→`/`\¬` artefacts appear in the PDF text. The headline statements as printed are not the statements. Fix: delete the `\renewcommand`, typeset Lean names with `\texttt{}` + `\seqsplit`/`url`-style breaking or `\lstinline`, and typeset the mathematical statements (`f(48)=8`, `A² = (q−1)I + J − D`, `A-REG → ¬ Erdos85Question`) in math mode with Lean names only in parentheses. Re-run `pdftotext` and grep for `\\_` before the next review. *(critical flag)*
- **§Cost to verify › cheaper route [L132] vs Evidence-levels table [L83] / Abstract [L35] / §8 [L313] — 1,159 vs 1,160.** "1,159 UNSAT verdicts needed 2,695 Kissat core-hours" contradicts the 1,160 whole-instance UNSAT rows stated everywhere else and in `refs/CENSUS.md`. Either one row's Kissat time is missing from the timing join (say so, and say which) or one of the two counts is wrong. Reconcile in one sentence and make the count agree with `CENSUS.md`. *(critical flag)*
