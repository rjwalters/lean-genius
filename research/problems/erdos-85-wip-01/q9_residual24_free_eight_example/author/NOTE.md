# Residual24 alone does not obstruct a free order-eight action

Take three orbits A_g,B_g,C_g indexed by g modulo8. Join A_g to A_(g+4), B_g, C_g; join B_g to C_(g+1),C_(g+2). The24-vertex graph is cubic and C4-free, and shifting all indices by1 is a free order-eight automorphism. Direct neighbor-set verification checks all276 distinct endpoint pairs and all24 action images. This construction is only a residual graph: it has not been extended to the attached/fixed vertices and does not satisfy or refute the other necessary full-graph constraints by itself.
