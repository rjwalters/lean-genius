# Independent reconstruction of the proposed N78/F10 exclusion

Status: PASS. Frozen primary proof matched byte-for-byte to the independently reviewed version; all four payload pins and accepted2220/2223/2225 premise hashes/statuses verified. The argument excludes N78/F10 and, using2220, leaves at most8 fixed vertices for any nonidentity involution. This is not a global N78 exclusion.

1. In E=8I+J-M², all unordered residual pairs with a G common neighbour are counted exactly once. Attached middles supply2 per B/C group, residual middles supply2*choose(d_j,2) per quotient index, and fixed middles supply0. With a=4+sumd, the degree sum in the5-regular E gives e_E(R,attached)=2sum d_j(d_j-4). Each binary4x4 row degree is0..4. Thus every d is0 or4. A degree4 row and symmetry force all four row degrees4, contradicting a<=10. Therefore all d=0, a=4, and there are no E edges from R to attached vertices. Each residual orbit is missed by exactly one typeA group.

2. For distinct fixed vertices, moved common neighbours cannot occur, since every moved vertex has at most one fixed neighbour. Thus E on the fixed set equals2I+J-H²; H cubic gives row sum3. TypeA centres use their two remaining E neighbours in the missed residual orbit and have none attached. Other centres have exactly two attached E neighbours. A typeB centre's E-neighbours are its internal unmatched pair. A typeC centre's are the unmatched pair in the unique deficient partner group. Both pairs lie in S, the attached vertices of R-degree2. In the typeC case the partner is also typeC, so those missed vertices indeed have R-degree2. Each pair's four R-neighbours occupy two entire distinct residual orbits, with no overlaps, by the2-orbit matching condition in2225.

3. Expand ME=EM at(fixed v,residual r). The ME side only uses H-neighbours of v whose typeA group misses r; its attached summands vanish because E(R,attached)=0. There is exactly one such candidate typeA centre per residual orbit. The EM side is0 for v in FA, and for v in FD it is the incidence from the special attached pair just identified:1 on two residual orbits and0 on the other two. Hence FA is independent, and each FD centre has exactly two FA neighbours and one FD neighbour. FA has4 vertices and FD has6. No classification of cubic10 graphs is needed.

4. S has12 vertices, two from each of six B/C groups. The remaining48 attached vertices T have R-degree1; split T into24 in A groups and24 in D groups. For an attached x of R-degree k=1or2, its attached degree is8-k. All8 residual vertices have exactly one common neighbour with x, because E(R,attached)=0. Summing these codegrees over R, fixed middles contribute0 and residual middles contribute0 (R independent); attached middles contribute their R-degrees. These common neighbours are exactly its S neighbours, each providing one other residual endpoint; no duplicates are possible. Consequently each R vertex has3 S neighbours,3 TA neighbours (the three A groups that do not miss it), and3 TD neighbours.
