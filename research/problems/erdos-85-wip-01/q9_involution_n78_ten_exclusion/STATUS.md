# N78 involutions cannot fix ten vertices

Accepted review2236 excludes the complete ten-fixed-vertex involution case for a hypothetical C4-free 78-vertex graph of minimum degree9. The deficiency degree identity forces the residual eight vertices independent. Commutation and codegree counts then force six equitable classes, giving thirteen two-step walks into a twelve-vertex class, contradicting C4-freeness. Together with accepted2220, every nonidentity involution fixes at most eight vertices.

The author proof, exact arithmetic audit, primary independent review, and second independent graph derivation are preserved. This is a paper proof with exact arithmetic, not a full Lean formalization, a global 78-vertex exclusion, or a solution of Erdos85.
