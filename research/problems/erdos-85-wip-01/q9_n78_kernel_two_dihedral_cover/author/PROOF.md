# Eight central extension laws cover the remaining kernel-two dihedral images

Assume the order16 matching normal form2419 and S-orbits2+4, with kernel K of order2 on S and dihedral image D8. The image-cover theorem2450 gives the standard square action on S4 and one of its three nontrivial characters on S2. This packet is conditional on that image; it does not need the later exclusion of the two product images.

Write K=<z>, central of order2. Choose lifts r,s of a quarter-turn and a vertex-axis reflection of the square. Because the quotient satisfies rbar^4=sbar^2=1 and sbar rbar sbar^-1=rbar^-1, there are bits a,b,c with r^4=z^a, s^2=z^b and srs^-1=r^-1 z^c. These relations are necessary for every such extension. Every group element has a unique normal form r^i s^j z^e for 0<=i<4 and binary j,e, because its quotient fixes i,j and the remaining kernel fixes e.

For x=(i,j,e) and y=(k,l,f), put n=i+(-1)^j k. Their product in normal form is

    (n mod4, j+l mod2, e+f+c*j*k+b*j*l+a*floor(n/4) mod2).

The floor term is also used for negative n, since r^n=r^(n mod4) z^(a*floor(n/4)). Thus the eight bit triples give an upper cover of every possible multiplication law with the chosen lifts. The checker verifies identity, inverses and every associativity triple for every table; all eight are valid. It retains all labels without an isomorphism quotient.

For each table the three character choices (u,v)=(0,1),(1,0),(1,1) act on S2 by i*u+j*v mod2. The S4 action is x -> i+(-1)^j*x mod4. Each combined S6 action is checked on all products and has kernel exactly {1,z}. These are precisely the three dihedral graph images in2450. The matching on S consists of S2 and the opposite pairs in the square.

An outside orbit of size8 has stabilizer generated by an involution t outside H, the S2 stabilizer, because H acts freely outside S by2419. The checker lists every conjugacy class of such involutions. A class representative fixes |C_A(t)|/2 points on its own coset action A/<t>. It also fixes the explicitly computed number of S points. Their sum must be at most six by2257. The table marks this necessary condition for every class; an admissible mark does not assert that its fixed graph has the required shape or that it extends to the desired graph. Since all points outside S have orbit sizes8 or16, these classes cover all potential eight-orbit stabilizers before further geometric restrictions.

The original30-second group-only domain completes with eight tables and24 labeled center actions. No graph incidences or combinations of vertex orbits are enumerated here. This is an exact abstract action cover, not a graph realization or a full dihedral-image exclusion. No capped domain is retried, and no global or Lean conclusion is claimed.
