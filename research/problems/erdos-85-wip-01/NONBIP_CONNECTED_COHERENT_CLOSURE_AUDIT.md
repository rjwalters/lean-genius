# NONBIP-CONNECTED coherent-closure audit

Date: 2026-08-26.  Node: `A-REG-NONBIP / NONBIP-CONNECTED [q]`.
Status: **generic 2-WL homogeneity mechanism cut; q=4 calibration recorded**.

## Candidate

Let `W(A)` be the coherent/cellular algebra generated by the ambient adjacency
matrix `A`: close under matrix multiplication, transpose, Hadamard product,
and extraction of diagonal relations.  Both

```text
D = (q-1)I + J - A^2
```

and the diagonal statistic

```text
2t_x = (A^3)_{xx}
```

belong to this closure.  The proposed force was that feasibility or
divisibility of the full coherent configuration might make its diagonal
fibers homogeneous along the connected graph `D`, and then constrain `At`
modulo four.

## Full q=4 calibration

`nonbip_connected_coherent_closure_q4.py` implements stable 2-WL refinement
on ordered vertex pairs, starting only from equality, adjacency, and
nonadjacency.  It enumerated 256 labelled q=4 boundary controls with a fixed
root neighborhood.  Every model has the same output profile:

```text
coherent rank: 18

diagonal fiber 1: size 8, t=1 on all vertices, At=6 on all vertices
diagonal fiber 2: size 8, t=2 on all vertices, At=6 on all vertices

D basis-relation sizes within the t=1 fiber: 8, 16
D basis-relation sizes within the t=2 fiber: 8, 16
```

Thus the closure recognizes both rooted statistics perfectly in calibration.
The two diagonal fibers are exactly the two eight-vertex defect components,
and the target mass congruence is visible as `At=6=2 (mod 4)`.

## Recognition is not a force

The first recognition is automatic, not a restriction.  Every matrix in a
coherent algebra has constant diagonal on each diagonal fiber.  Since `A^3`
belongs to `W(A)`, the fibers necessarily refine the exact values of `t_x`.
Likewise, once the diagonal matrix `T=diag(t)` has been extracted, the vector

```text
A T 1 = At
```

is constant on diagonal fibers.  This says that 2-WL *colors* vertices by the
desired data; it supplies no prescribed value for those colors.

Nor does connectedness of a union of basis relations imply that a coherent
configuration has one diagonal fiber.  Basis relations routinely run between
distinct source and target fibers.  If `D` had an edge between vertices with
different `t mod 4`, coherent refinement would simply place that edge in a
relation from one already-distinguished fiber to the other.  All source and
target valency/intersection-number equations remain meaningful; the axioms do
not prohibit such a relation.  Therefore the statement

```text
D has no basis relation joining unequal t mod 4 fibers
```

is exactly the desired defect-edge propagation theorem, not a consequence of
the definition of coherent closure.

The q=4 profile cannot close this gap because its defect graph is disconnected:
the observed absence of cross-fiber D relations is precisely its already-known
componentwise constancy.  Extrapolating that absence to connected q>=8 models
would assume the sharp terminal.

## Verdict

Ordinary coherent closure / 2-WL is cut as an independent mechanism.  It
canonically packages `t` and `At`, and the q=4 controls give a clean rank-18
calibration, but cellular-algebra feasibility does not make a connected union
of relations homogeneous and does not prescribe the fiber value of `At`
modulo four.  A viable association-scheme successor must prove a new
q-specific nonexistence theorem for cross-fiber D relations (for example from
Krein positivity or dyadic divisibility).  Merely computing the closure or
naming its intersection numbers encodes the two terminal claims rather than
deriving them.
