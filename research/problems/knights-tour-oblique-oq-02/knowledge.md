### Why the seeker's tractability=7 is the right estimate

The structural targets (support, D4-mod-8, reversal-mod-2) are reachable
with the existing infrastructure and standard Mathlib finite-group-action
API (`MulAction`, `Quotient`, `orbit_card`). None of them require
enumeration. The full histogram is unreachable, so a successful OQ-02
deliverable is the **structural skeleton**, not the histogram. This
matches the seeker's "structural extension" framing.

### Honest assessment of contribution boundary

This problem is *not* solving an open mathematical question — Knuth has
already enumerated the histogram externally. The Lean contribution is
**formalizing the structural symmetries of the distribution** in a way
that exposes (rather than hides) the dependency on the external
enumeration. The "interesting" Lean theorem is the D4-mod-8 lemma plus the
winding-parity constraint; the "uninteresting" but necessary work is
defining `obliqueDistribution` and packaging it.
