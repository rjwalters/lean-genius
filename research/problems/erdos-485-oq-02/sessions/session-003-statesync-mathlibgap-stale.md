### Recipe C — Explore Plünnecke-Ruzsa for cancellation control

The cancellation barrier (described in Part VII of `Erdos485OQ02.lean`)
asks: can cancellation be bounded sublinearly in `|A + A|`? Plünnecke-Ruzsa
inequalities (now in `Mathlib/Combinatorics/Additive/PluenneckeRuzsa.lean`
at v4.26.0) bound iterated sumsets like `|nA - mA|` in terms of `|A + A| /
|A|` (the doubling constant). 

**Possible angle**: if `A = support(P)` has small doubling, then `A + A`
has structure (Freiman's theorem) — but cancellation control still
requires bounding **coefficient values**, not just supports. The
combinatorial obstacle survives.

**Realistic outcome**: a *negative result* showing Plünnecke-Ruzsa alone
cannot bridge the gap. This itself would be a valuable formalization
contribution.
